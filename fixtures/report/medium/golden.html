Executive Summary

Over the April window, modeled Claude Code spend was $671.28 across 44 sessions in 7 repositories, consuming 961.9M tokens. Work spanned three source orgs: the employer org northwind-media ($353.04 over 20 sessions, four repos), the open-source org openpipe-oss ($264.78 over 17 sessions in one repo, quill), and Jordan's personal org jrivera ($53.44 over 7 sessions in two repos).

What This Funded

Employer org: northwind-media

$353.04 over 20 sessions across four repos. In beacon ($165.84), the summaries describe ingest reliability work: session 14fabbe1 added a dead-letter queue so a parser-rejected record is parked with its failure reason instead of retried forever; 4b1d68f6 traced a cold start to a synchronous hostname lookup and moved it behind a warmed cache; 54f9d4bb audited the retention policy against what the storage layer actually deletes. In tideline ($83.29), 18c8897c ported the dashboard to new theme tokens and f01b33b8 profiled the slow dashboard query and added a missing composite index. In halyard ($58.42), the rollout-plan and rollback-trigger sessions (07dce623, 2ad5c741) drafted a staged cutover and wired its rollback to the existing health check. In almanac ($45.49), 519daf85 added cursor pagination to the list endpoint and f9e95abd repaired the nightly backfill to resume from the last completed partition.

Personal and open-source: openpipe-oss and jrivera

openpipe-oss/quill ($264.78, 17 sessions) dominated the open-source tier. Two summary themes repeat: hardening the release script (session 05981637 made it refuse to publish on a dirty tree, an existing tag, or a missing changelog entry) and documenting the plugin hooks against the code rather than the wiki, dropping two hooks the loader no longer calls (2dc73697, 3aed06b6 and others). In Jordan's personal org jrivera ($53.44, two repos): driftwood parser work (session 86b0fd93 taught the parser to carry a line and column into its error type; 93071145 split it into a tokenizer and a shape builder) and sextant test work (4bf28f14 rewrote the snapshot harness to compare structurally rather than by rendered string).
